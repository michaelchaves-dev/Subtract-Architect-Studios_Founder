# Consulting

## The center of Subtract Architect Studios

Consulting is the primary bridge between the studio's research, software, and real-world client outcomes.

We help organizations determine where AI and automation genuinely improve operations — and where they create unnecessary expense, complexity, security exposure, or operational risk.

## Engagement model

A typical engagement follows a subtraction-first sequence:

1. **Baseline** — understand current systems, costs, workflows, pain points, and constraints.
2. **Subtract** — remove unnecessary tools, calls, tokens, steps, permissions, and duplicated work.
3. **Protect** — establish security, authority, provenance, and rollback boundaries.
4. **Route** — choose the cheapest-capable models, tools, and execution paths.
5. **Build** — create only what the remaining problem actually requires.
6. **Measure** — compare real cost, usage, reliability, latency, and operational outcomes.
7. **Train** — make sure the client's team can operate and understand the system.
8. **Refine** — continue improving only where measured evidence justifies change.

## Pricing philosophy

The studio does not want an economic incentive to make a client's AI system consume more tokens.

Depending on the engagement, commercial structures can include:

- defined consulting scopes
- project pricing
- tiered subscriptions
- retainers
- implementation + support
- performance / savings-aligned structures when measurable baselines make them appropriate

The governing principle is the **Honesty Guarantee**: if a simpler, less expensive, or existing solution is sufficient, that should be said plainly.

## What success looks like

A successful engagement should leave the client with some combination of:

- lower AI/inference cost
- fewer unnecessary tokens
- fewer tools or duplicated workflows
- stronger security
- clearer human authority
- better reliability
- better reasoning and verification
- measurable savings
- improved staff capability
- a system that is easier to understand and maintain

**The objective is not maximum automation. It is maximum useful efficiency with acceptable risk.**
