# Research & Education

Subtract Architect Studios treats research and education as part of the same mission as consulting: improve how people build, understand, evaluate, and operate intelligent systems.

## Frontier-model research

### Frontier Faults
https://github.com/michaelchaves-dev/Frontier_Faults

Frontier Faults records concrete issues observed while working with frontier AI systems. The goal is not vendor-bashing. The goal is to turn recurring model problems into useful evidence for better design.

Research themes include:

- behavioral consistency
- instruction drift
- hallucination and unsupported certainty
- scope/goalpost shifts
- tool-use reliability
- safety and authority boundaries
- memory/persistence behavior
- agent coordination
- latency/cost tradeoffs
- model-to-model disagreement
- reproducibility of failures and fixes

## Memory & intelligence research

### ZeroFold
ZeroFold explores the idea that intelligence does not require all available context to remain unfolded at all times.

Key questions:

- What information is actually durable?
- What can be safely discarded?
- What should be folded into compact state?
- When should memory be promoted?
- How can provenance survive compression?
- Does stored memory repay its creation/retrieval cost?
- What minimum model capability does a task actually require?

### Ternary reasoning
Ternary reasoning keeps uncertainty visible instead of forcing every claim into true/false or yes/no.

Typical state:
- +1 / support
- 0 / unknown, neutral, or unresolved
- -1 / counter-evidence

### Negative Step Zero
Before optimization or execution, validate the premise, source, authority, value, and need for the task itself.

### RelAi Race
For consequential outputs, use independent challenge paths so one model, compression process, or validator does not certify itself.

### QST — Quantum Subtraction Theory
QST is a distinct Subtract Architect Studios methodology exploring subtraction-first reasoning, state reduction, conservation, and alternative computational/logic structures.

## DAR — Daily DeFi Market & Analysis Report

DAR turns multiple DeFi/market indicators into a recurring, structured operator report.

The research objective is broader than producing another crypto dashboard: it is an experiment in turning noisy, fragmented, fast-moving information into a repeatable decision-support format with provenance, consistent metrics, and human-readable interpretation.
