## Operating principles

### KISS — Keep It Simple, Stupid
Complexity must justify its existence.

### Subtraction Before Addition
Before adding another model, agent, tool, workflow, or subscription, ask what can be removed, reused, folded, cached, consolidated, or routed more intelligently.

### Triple-Check Standard
Important claims, calculations, outputs, system decisions, and security assumptions deserve independent verification.

### Honesty Guarantee
If something is unnecessary, unproven, not ready, not saving money, or not working as expected, say so. Accuracy is more valuable than protecting a narrative.

### Human Authority
Automation should increase human capability, not quietly remove human control. High-impact actions require explicit authority, visible state, and reversible checkpoints.

### Measurable Economics
Optimization should be visible in actual usage, tokens, inference spend, latency, reliability, and operational outcomes.

### Aligned Pricing
Where the engagement supports it, we favor predictable tiers, consulting retainers, and savings/value-aligned pricing rather than creating incentives to charge clients for every additional token consumed.

## ZeroFold
**Fold information to its minimum useful state. Unfold only what intelligence requires.**

# Methods & research concepts

Representative methods developed or used across the studio include:

### Negative Step Zero
Before solving the stated problem, determine whether the premise, task, source, cost, or proposed solution should be trusted at all.

### RelAi Race
Use independent paths or models to challenge important outputs instead of allowing one system to certify itself.

### Ternary Reasoning
Use positive / neutral / negative or support / unknown / counter-evidence states to avoid forcing uncertain evidence into binary conclusions.

### QST — Quantum Subtraction Theory
A distinct Subtract Architect Studios methodology exploring subtraction-first reasoning, state reduction, information conservation, and alternative ways to structure computational and reasoning problems.

### ZeroFold / Zero Atoms
Preserve durable knowledge in compact, deduplicated, provenance-aware forms and expand detail only when a task requires it.

### Reversible Checkpoints
Prefer architectures where important changes can be inspected, stopped, rolled back, or reconstructed.
